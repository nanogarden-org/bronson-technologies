AI Workflow Boundary Checklist

A compact review aid for checking what an AI workflow knows, what it is asking to do, and what authority exists before it crosses into external action.

Status access path under reviewDelivery not publicly distributed

WHAT IT WOULD HELP REVIEW

Keep evidence, interpretation, authority, and action separate.

This proposed checklist is for builders, operators, and small teams reviewing an AI workflow before it sends, changes, approves, or triggers something outside the model boundary.

  • Source and transformation

    Identify the evidence and the changes that produced the current interpretation.

  • Requested capability

    Name the external action rather than treating a fluent answer as the action itself.

  • Authority and failure path

    Make the approval boundary, refusal path, and stale-or-contradictory evidence case visible.

  • Record after action

    Preserve enough context for a later reviewer to understand what happened.
