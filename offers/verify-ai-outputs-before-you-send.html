Verify Your AI Outputs Before You Send

A pre-send review aid for messages, documents, code, decisions, and other outputs that may leave your workspace or trigger a real-world consequence.

Status access path under reviewDelivery not publicly distributed

WHAT IT WOULD HELP CHECK

Fluent is not the same as ready.

The proposed review pass helps a person inspect the output at the moment where an AI suggestion becomes something another person may read, trust, execute, or receive.

  • Claim and evidence

    Check whether the important statements are supported, current, and within the intended scope.

  • Recipient and context

    Confirm that the audience, tone, attachments, links, and surrounding context are correct.

  • Instruction and authority

    Separate what the output recommends from what the sender is actually allowed to do.

  • Irreversibility and review

    Pause before an output creates a commitment, disclosure, transaction, or other difficult-to-reverse effect.

ACCESS BOUNDARY

Review aid, not certification.

The full pre-send checklist is intentionally not published on this page. This offer is being tested as a practical entry point for responsible AI use; it does not provide legal, medical, security, or client-specific assurance.
