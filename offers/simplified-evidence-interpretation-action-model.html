A starter teaching model for keeping evidence, interpretation, requested action, authorization, and the final record from collapsing into one fluent answer.

THE TEACHING LENS

A smaller model for a larger distinction.

The proposed resource is designed for people who need a first-pass way to inspect one AI workflow without adopting a full research architecture.

Evidence → Interpretation → Requested action → Authorization → Record

The extended version would provide examples and a compact worksheet for applying the lens to one real workflow. It intentionally leaves out identity systems, detailed provenance graphs, policy languages, security testing, legal instruments, conformance testing, and operational runbooks.

ACCESS BOUNDARY

Educational, not equivalent.

The full worksheet is intentionally not published on this page. This is an interest test for a practical offer, not a claim of production readiness or a replacement for TurtleML, LCA-MVP, or a formal governance program.
