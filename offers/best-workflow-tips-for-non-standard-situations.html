Best Workflow Tips for Non-Standard Situations

A practical offer for the cases that do not fit the happy path: ambiguous inputs, unusual requests, missing evidence, conflicting instructions, and decisions that need a deliberate pause.

Status access path under reviewDelivery not publicly distributed

WHAT IT WOULD COVER

Design the exception before it becomes the failure.

The proposed tips are short patterns for making non-standard situations inspectable rather than forcing them through a normal workflow that was never designed for them.

  • Name the exception

    State what makes the case different before the workflow chooses a path.

  • Preserve the evidence

    Keep the source, uncertainty, and missing context visible instead of smoothing them away.

  • Choose a stop or escalation point

    Define when the system should pause, ask, refuse, or hand the case to a person.

  • Record the reason

    Leave a compact explanation of why the unusual path was accepted or rejected.

ACCESS BOUNDARY

Not a generic productivity list.

The full set of tips is intentionally not published on this page. The offer is being tested as a focused teaching resource for AI workflows, not as a universal operating procedure or a guarantee of safe outcomes.
