# Evidence policy

- Distinguish claims, implementation evidence, credentials, and third-party testimony.
- Do not convert historical IT evidence into retroactive AI expertise.
- Preserve source wording, date, and provenance for historical evidence.
- Link credentials to issuer or external validators whenever possible.
- Prefer repository code, tests, schemas, release records, and working artifacts for capability claims.
- Mark active-development and experimental projects clearly.
- Keep one canonical structured record (`proof-map.yaml`) and derive human/machine views from it.
