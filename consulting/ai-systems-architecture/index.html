SERVICE 01 · ARCHITECTURE

Understand the system you already have—and the one you actually need.

Independent architectural assessment for organizations combining AI models, agents, APIs, business applications, data sources, and human workflows.

TYPICAL QUESTIONS

Where do the boundaries fail?

  • Which systems exchange information, and through which interfaces?
  • Where are identity, permissions, and delegated authority established?
  • Which system is authoritative for each important record or decision?
  • Where can context, provenance, or state be lost during handoffs?
  • What happens when a vendor, model, API, or integration changes?
  • Which controls are enforced technically, and which exist only in policy or expectation?

POSSIBLE OUTPUTS

Decision-ready architecture evidence

  • Current-state system and interface map.
  • Data-flow, trust-boundary, and authority map.
  • Dependency and failure-mode register.
  • Target-state options with trade-offs and assumptions.
  • Prioritized remediation or implementation roadmap.

SCOPE BOUNDARY

Platform-independent by design

Assessment can cover existing environments using Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic, internal systems, third-party APIs, or mixed infrastructure. Familiarity with a vendor is not a requirement to recommend its adoption.

This service is architectural analysis, not a security certification, penetration test, legal opinion, or guarantee of regulatory compliance.
