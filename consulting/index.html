CROSS-DOMAIN · PLATFORM-INDEPENDENT

Make AI systems useful without giving them unbounded authority.

Architecture and engineering support for organizations working across AI tools, business platforms, data sources, and human decision-makers. The work focuses on the boundaries between systems: information, permissions, privacy, provenance, verification, and accountability.

You do not need to adopt a Bronson Technologies architecture. Work can begin with the tools, vendors, workflows, and constraints you already have. The goal is to understand how they interact, where authority and information cross boundaries, and what should change.
