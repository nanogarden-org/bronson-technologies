SERVICE 03 · ENGINEERING

Turn a specific workflow problem into a testable implementation.

Applied prototyping, integration design, and workflow improvement for teams that need to test an AI-enabled process before committing to broader deployment.

ENGINEERING APPROACH

Smallest useful, inspectable implementation

  1. Choose one workflow and define its intended outcome.
  2. Identify source data, interfaces, users, permissions, and prohibited actions.
  3. Define success criteria, failure conditions, and human review points.
  4. Build a bounded prototype using appropriate existing tools.
  5. Test normal paths, edge cases, malformed inputs, conflicting information, and interrupted execution.
  6. Record limitations, dependencies, and what would be required before broader use.

POSSIBLE OUTPUTS

Practical deliverables

  • Runnable proof of concept or workflow demonstration.
  • Interface and integration specification.
  • Test cases and observed results.
  • Operational handoff notes and known limitations.
  • Next-stage recommendation: stop, revise, extend, or pilot.

WHAT THIS IS NOT

A prototype is not production.

A bounded PoC demonstrates selected behavior under stated conditions. It does not automatically establish security, reliability, compliance, scalability, clinical validity, or suitability for unsupervised operation.

Customer platforms and constraints determine the implementation. Bronson Technologies seed architectures may inform the design, but adoption of those architectures is never a prerequisite.
