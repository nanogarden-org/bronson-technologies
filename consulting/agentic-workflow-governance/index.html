SERVICE 02 · GOVERNANCE

Make the agent's authority explicit before it acts.

Design and review of controls around AI agents and automated workflows: what they can observe, access, infer, change, delegate, and execute—and where human authorization remains necessary.

REVIEW DIMENSIONS

Govern the complete action path

  • Identity and scope: Which agent, user, service identity, or integration is acting?
  • Access and privacy: Which data can be accessed, for what purpose, and under what duration or context?
  • Delegation: Can an agent pass authority to another agent or tool? Can that authority be narrowed or revoked?
  • Action boundaries: Which actions are read-only, reversible, consequential, or prohibited?
  • Verification: What independent checks must pass before an output or action is accepted?
  • Human accountability: Who can approve, reject, override, or review consequential outcomes?
  • Continuity: Can the organization reconstruct inputs, transformations, permissions, and actions later?
  • Failure and recovery: What happens with stale context, conflicting evidence, outages, or partial execution?

POSSIBLE OUTPUTS

Controls that can be inspected

  • Agent capability and authority matrix.
  • Data-access and privacy boundary map.
  • Human-approval and escalation model.
  • Verification and audit-event requirements.
  • Exception, revocation, and recovery scenarios.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Governance is not a badge.

A policy statement does not prove a control is enforced. A platform feature does not prove it is configured correctly. A successful demonstration does not establish production safety. Reviews distinguish documented policy, technical enforcement, observed behavior, and independently verified outcomes.

Work can be informed by high-consequence domains without claiming clinical, legal, security, or regulatory authority.
