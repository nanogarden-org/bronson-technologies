# ABCI Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Corpus accountability

- Preserve archive inventories and hashes.
- Separate platform specification from NeuroCrown embodiment.
- Mark legal/safety boundaries.

## Phase 1 — Runnable MVP

- Simulated adapter.
- CSV adapter.
- Typed state claim schema.
- Permission policy checks.
- Expiry/lifetime checks.
- One demo app.

## Phase 2 — Reference integrations

- BLE placeholder adapter.
- NeuroCrown reference adapter.
- HRV/GSR/EMG data examples.
- Local-only storage mode.

## Phase 3 — Validation

- Protocol templates.
- Within-subject repeatability tests.
- Artifact detection tests.
- Multimodal vs single-modality comparisons.

## Phase 4 — Public/platform decision

- Decide license.
- Decide whether to split into `nanogarden-org/abci`.
- Decide whether NeuroCrown gets its own repo.
