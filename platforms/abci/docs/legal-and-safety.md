# Legal, Safety, and Claims Boundary

**Not legal advice.** This document is an internal research and publication boundary for ABCI / NeuroCrown materials. A qualified attorney should review any public release, commercial use, fundraising claim, medical claim, patent filing, data-collection activity, or human-subject study.

## Current public-safe claim class

ABCI may be described as:

> An early-stage, hardware-agnostic architecture for local-first physiological signal routing, typed state representation, provenance, permissions, and adaptive human-machine interfaces.

NeuroCrown may be described as:

> A proposed/reference wearable ecosystem intended to explore ABCI-compatible sensing and feedback using commercially available components.

## Claims to avoid until validated

Do not publicly claim that ABCI or NeuroCrown:

- Diagnoses, treats, mitigates, or prevents any disease or disorder.
- Reliably detects emotions, intentions, consciousness states, spiritual states, or mental health conditions.
- Provides validated authentication security against real attackers.
- Provides clinical-grade EEG, BCI, or neurofeedback performance.
- Is equivalent to implantable BCI systems.
- Has been validated in human-subject research unless an actual documented study exists.

## Required disclaimers for demos

Use language like:

> This demonstration uses simulated or experimental physiological data. Outputs are software-state examples, not medical, psychological, legal, employment, or security determinations.

## Human-subject and privacy boundary

Any collection of real physiological data from people other than the author should have:

- written informed consent,
- data-retention limits,
- deletion procedure,
- statement of non-medical purpose,
- safety stop conditions,
- no coercive participation,
- no employment/insurance/credit/eligibility use,
- no hidden raw-signal export.

## IP/provenance posture

The corpus inventory preserves file names, dates, sizes, and SHA-256 hashes to establish what artifacts were present in the supplied archives. It does not prove originality by itself; it supports chronology and accountability. Git commits add another public/private timestamp layer but do not replace formal IP counsel.

## Publication posture

Recommended initial status: **private repo / research-preview / no medical claims / no security claims beyond architectural intent.**
